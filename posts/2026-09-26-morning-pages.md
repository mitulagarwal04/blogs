# Morning pages, again

Woke up, kettle on, notebook open. No phone for the first 40 minutes.

What I noticed after a week:

- **First page is garbage.** Complaints, groceries, noise. That's the point — drain it.
- **Second page turns.** An idea from yesterday resurfaces, calmer.
- **Third page decides.** One small thing to do today. Just one.

> The rule I kept: pen moves, no editing, no re-reading until Sunday.

Today's one thing: ship this blog. So here it is.

**Stack check:** slept 7h, coffee 1x, steps 0 (yet).

Tomorrow: same pages, then a walk before code.
