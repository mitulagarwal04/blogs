# Readings: why small blogs outlive threads

Read: *“Write your own timeline”* — the usual indie-web sermon, but one line stuck:

> Post it on your domain first. Syndicate everywhere else.

So this is the new rule for this log:

- **tweets** → 2–3 line notes, posted here, not on someone's algorithm
- **readings** → link + one-line why it matters, no bookmark graveyard
- **building / learning** → the longer 300-word entries

Today's reading list:

1. FastAPI docs on response classes — `TemplateResponse(request, ...)` bit me once already.
2. The `design.md` color tokens — one yellow (`#FFD21E`) does all the shouting.

Total time: 25 min. Notes: 2. Threads posted elsewhere: 0.
