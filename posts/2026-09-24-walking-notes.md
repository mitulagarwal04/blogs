# Walking notes: the sticker test

45-minute walk, one question: why does the Jev Decision Index look *fun* while most dashboards look tired?

Answer from `design.md`:

- **2px ink outlines everywhere.** Cards, chips, buttons. Nothing floats vaguely.
- **Hard shadows, no blur.** `4px 4px 0 #232323`. It pops like paper cutouts.
- **One yellow.** `#FFD21E` only for active / primary / sorted. Restraint makes it sing.
- **Chewy for numbers, mono for labels.** Playful + precise in one glance.

> My test now: screenshot any component, print it mentally on cream `#E9E6DD`. Would it survive as a sticker? If not, add ink or remove blur.

Applied here: cream bg, off-white `#FBF8F1` cards, pill chips, yellow `<mark>` in the H1.

Walking total: 6,214 steps. Ideas: 3. Good trade.
